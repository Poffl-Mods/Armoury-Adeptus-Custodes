# Armoury: Adeptus Custodes

A feature-complete Custodes armoury mod for *Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader*.

Wield four iconic, carefully balanced pieces of Custodes wargear with custom models,
inventory icons, versatile combat styles, unique abilities, and six level-scaling
variants each:

- **Guardian Spear** — a two-handed melee and ranged bolter hybrid
- **Castellan Axe** — a heavy execution weapon with melee control and a precise bolt shot
- **Sentinel Sword** — a mobile, defensive power sword with an overchargeable power field
- **Praesidium Shield** — an auric bulwark that protects allies and suppresses psykers

No Deathwatch, MicroPatches, Harmony, or other mod dependency is required.

## Installation

Download **Adeptus Custodes v1.4.0.zip** from the
[latest GitHub release](https://github.com/Poffl-Mods/Armoury-Adeptus-Custodes/releases/tag/v1.4.0),
install it with Modfinder for Rogue Trader, and enable **Armoury: Adeptus Custodes**.

## Current version

`1.4.0`

Version 1.4.0 completes the originally planned armoury. It adds the Castellan Axe,
finishes all four equipment progressions, removes the Praesidium Shield's inherited
class and faction restrictions, and places every V1 item in a persistent in-game
loot chest. ToyBox is no longer required for a new campaign.

## Acquisition

The four V1 items can be acquired naturally during the Prologue. After opening the
gate leading to the **Warrant of Trade**, enter the chamber containing the ancient
document. A distinctive black-and-gold Custodes chest stands near the large
ceremonial floor emblem.

The chest contains:

- **Custodian's Vigil** — Guardian Spear
- **Custodian's Edge** — Sentinel Sword
- **Custodian's Aegis** — Praesidium Shield
- **Castellan's Vigil** — Castellan Axe

Only the first variant needs to be obtained. Outside combat, each item is
automatically replaced when its wielder reaches levels 16, 26, 36, 44, and 50.
Upgrades never occur during combat.

## Guardian Spear

The signature weapon of the Adeptus Custodes is a master-crafted fusion of power
blade and bolt weapon. It can cut through armoured enemies in melee, sweep across a
wide seven-cell arc, or engage distant targets with its integrated bolter.

- **Strike** — focused standard melee attack
- **Cleave** — broad seven-cell melee sweep
- **Bolt Shot** — accurate single ranged shot
- **Bolt Burst** — multi-shot bolter burst with scaling burst size and magazine

Its Weapon Skill, Ballistic Skill, parry chance, critical chance, and critical damage
bonuses improve across all six variants.

## Castellan Axe

The Castellan Axe is a brutal Custodes hybrid weapon built around raw single-target
damage, battlefield control, and decisive ranged execution.

- **Strike** — heavy standard melee attack with strong armour penetration
- **Castellan Onslaught** — two separately resolved hits, increasing to three from V4
- **Castellan Sweep** — compact three-cell area attack
- **Executioner Bolt** — precise long-range shot with no ammunition and a two-round cooldown
- **Haft Strike** — 1 AP control attack that pushes one cell and can stun for one round

## Sentinel Sword

The Sentinel Sword is a one-handed power weapon focused on precision, mobility, and
defence. It pairs naturally with the Praesidium Shield while remaining effective on
its own.

- **Strike** — precise standard melee attack
- **Sentinel Onslaught** — rapid sequence of separately resolved attacks
- **Sentinel Cleave** — compact three-cell area attack
- **Power Field** — adds scaling bonus damage for an increasing duration; V6 lasts until combat ends
- **Sentinel Wave** — 1 AP energy wave with a five-cell range and two-round cooldown

Its Weapon Skill, parry chance, and dodge chance improve across all six variants.

## Praesidium Shield

The Praesidium Shield is an immense auric bulwark with a constant 50% block chance.
It can be equipped by any character without class or faction requirements.

- **Shield Bash** — adjacent attack with damage that increases through every tier
- **Auric Bastion** — for one round, the wielder and allies within two cells receive
  individual force shields that nullify all incoming damage
- **Null Shockwave** — for one round, every unit within seven cells, including allies,
  is unable to use Psychic Power abilities; weapon, item, and Navigator abilities remain available

From V2 onward, the shield also grants increasing Strength and Toughness bonuses,
reaching +10 to both characteristics at V6.

campaign has already passed this point, add the V1 items through ToyBox's
