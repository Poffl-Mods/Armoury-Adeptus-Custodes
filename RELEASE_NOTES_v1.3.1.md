# Armoury: Adeptus Custodes — Version 1.3.1

Version 1.3.1 fixes ability cooldowns and retunes weapon progression so Custodes
weapons remain deliberately powerful without trivialising campaign encounters.

## Fixes

- **Activate Power Field** now correctly enters its documented 7-round cooldown after use.
- The fix applies to all six Sentinel Sword variants.
- **Auric Bastion** and **Null Shockwave** now correctly enter their documented cooldowns.
- The shield ability fixes apply to all six Praesidium Shield variants.
- Auric Bastion's damage cancellation was verified against both melee and ranged attacks;
  its protective effect itself did not require a change.

## Balance

- Weapon progression now follows the campaign and archetype bands: levels 1–15, 16–25,
  26–35, 36–43, 44–49, and 50–55.
- Guardian Spear and Sentinel Sword damage has been rebalanced against representative
  vanilla weapons from Chapters 1–5 while preserving their stronger Custodes identity.
- Passive effects and the Praesidium Shield's defensive values are unchanged.
