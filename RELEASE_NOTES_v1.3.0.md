# Armoury: Adeptus Custodes — Version 1.3.0

Version 1.3.0 introduces the **Praesidium Shield**, new visual improvements for the existing Custodes weapons, and several equipment compatibility fixes.

## Praesidium Shield

The Praesidium Shield is now available in six automatically progressing variants, from **Custodian's Aegis** to **Aegis of the Emperor**. Like the Guardian Spear and Sentinel Sword, it upgrades automatically at levels 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50.

- **Shield Bash** deals increasing damage with each variant.
- The shield retains its defensive preparation ability and a 50% shield block chance.
- **Auric Bastion** projects a protective field around the wielder. The wielder and nearby allies each receive a visible force shield that blocks all incoming damage for one round.
- **Null Shockwave** releases a visible nullifying shockwave and prevents every nearby Psyker—ally or enemy—from using Psychic Power abilities for one round. Weapon, item, and Navigator abilities remain available.
- The cooldowns of Auric Bastion and Null Shockwave improve with each shield variant, from eight rounds at the first tier to three rounds at the final tier.
- Higher shield variants grant increasing Strength and Toughness bonuses.

The Praesidium Shield also has a custom model, inventory icon, ability icons, descriptions, activation animations, and a corrected back-mounted position when its weapon set is inactive.

## Weapon-set compatibility

The Praesidium Shield is now local to the weapon set in which it is equipped. It no longer occupies or blocks the off-hand slot of both weapon sets, allowing a two-handed weapon such as the Guardian Spear to remain equipped in the other set.

This behaviour applies to all six shield variants and is restored correctly after loading a saved game. The Guardian Spear remains a true two-handed weapon and cannot be combined with the shield in the same active set.

## Visual improvements

- Updated the Guardian Spear and Sentinel Sword materials to give their auric metal a brighter, polished sparkle instead of the previous matte appearance.
- Added a polished metallic finish to the Praesidium Shield.
- Improved weapon and shield positioning, including character-aware Guardian Spear back placement and corrected Praesidium Shield orientation and distance from the character's spine.
- Added dedicated inventory and ability icons, including the new Null Shockwave icon.

## Additional fixes and polish

- Added all six Praesidium Shield variants to automatic level progression.
- Added scaling Shield Bash damage and Strength/Toughness bonuses.
- Added tier-specific cooldowns and descriptions for the shield abilities.
- Fixed stale shield-slot overrides when loading saves with the Guardian Spear, Sentinel Sword, and Praesidium Shield equipped.
- Preserved the intended shield behaviour across male and female character models.
