# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Fixed the Guardian Spear clipping through characters while sheathed.
- Added automatic back placement based on the loaded character rig's `Spine_3` transform.
- Supports male, female, and compatible companion rigs without separate hard-coded profiles.
- Preserves the spear's tested upright orientation and height while keeping it close to the back.
- Reapplies the placement correctly when switching between held and sheathed states.
